## 🎯 本期主线：把旧表达变成「能照着说的脚本」

> 你的真实使用场景是**卖课、招生、跟家长对话**。下面 3 个脚本，每个只挑旧料里已验证的表达，串成一段能开口就说的话。

---

## 🗂 场景 A：给家长做诊断 / 首次沟通（招生第一步）

| 表达 | 中文 | 出处 / 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **It's one thing to…, it's another thing to…** | …是一回事，…又是另一回事 | 议论骨架王牌，说服力最强 |
| **What's at the root of that?** | 根源是什么 | 挖需求，不急着卖 |
| **dive into the details** | 深入细节 | 表示"我可以细讲"，显得专业 |
| **as promised** | 按之前说的 | 交付/兑现承诺时开场 |
| **Let's call it a day.** | 今天就到这吧 | 收尾不尴尬 |

**🗣 可直接说的一段：**
> Look, **it's one thing to** memorize words, **it's another thing to** actually say them out loud. Before I tell you what to do, let me ask — **what's at the root of that** for your kid? Is it that he's scared to speak, or that he has no words to say?
> Once we know that, I can **dive into the details** and **run a quick experiment** with one lesson. **As promised**, I'll send you the notes afterward. **Let's call it a day** here.

## 🗂 场景 B：被拒绝 / 家长说"再考虑一下"（心态 + 回话）

| 表达 | 中文 | 出处 / 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **not feeling a spark** | 没火花 | 形容"感觉不对"，比 no interest 温和 |
| **it doesn't work out** | 没成 | 感情/合作/计划通用 |
| **not right for me** | 不适合我 | 最体面的拒绝 |
| **get over sth** | 走出来 | get over rejection |
| **build resilience** | 建抗挫力 | build that resilience **muscle** |
| **There's no magic pill for this.** | 这事没有捷径 | 打碎"速成"幻想 |

**🗣 可直接说的一段：**
> If **it doesn't work out**, that's fine — maybe **it's just not right for you** right now, and **I'm not feeling a spark** either way. But **there's no magic pill for this**: nobody gets fluent in a month. What you can do is **build that resilience muscle** and keep showing up. **I wouldn't go back for anything.**

## 🗂 场景 C：催行动 / 鼓励报名（收口）

| 表达 | 中文 | 出处 / 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **go for it** | 大胆去争取 | 最短鼓励句 |
| **put yourself out there** | 主动出击 | ❌ put yourself out |
| **give it a shot / see how it goes** | 试一把 / 看情况 | 降低门槛 |
| **Chances are (that)…** | 很有可能 | 比 maybe 有判断感 |
| **I can guarantee that.** | 我保证 | 强断言收尾 |
| **stay small / it serves them** | 保持不起眼 / 对他们有利 | 分析"为什么不敢开口" |

**🗣 可直接说的一段：**
> A lot of people **stay small** because it **serves them** — nobody can laugh at you if you never speak. But **chances are**, after three weeks you'll wonder why you waited. So **put yourself out there**, **give it a shot**, and **see how it goes**. If the method is right, **I can guarantee that** you'll hear the difference. **Go for it.**

## 🔴 累积易错点（默写自测）

1. catch up **with** sb ｜ dive **into** the details ｜ experiment **with** sth
2. **used to do**（过去）≠ **be used to doing**（习惯）
3. **the** loneliness that comes with **choosing**（介词后动名词）
4. want / tell sb **to** do sth（不能省 to）
5. cancel **on** sb ｜ be into **sb** ｜ get **over** it
6. put yourself **out there**（不是 put yourself out）
7. **experiment**（实验）≠ **experience**（经验）
8. **It's one thing to do**, **it's another thing to do**（两边都要 to + 动词原形）
