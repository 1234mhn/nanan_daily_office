## 🗂 场景一：见面寒暄 / 找人聊天
> 最高频，先把这块练成肌肉记忆

| 表达 | 中文 | 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **How are you doing today?** | 你今天过得怎么样？ | 应答：It's going pretty good. / Not too bad. |
| **catch up with sb** | 和某人聊近况 | 别丢 with；catch up on sth = 补上某事 |
| **guess what** | 你猜怎么着 | 引出八卦/反转，≈ here's the thing |
| **people don't really talk about…** | 很少有人真正谈… | 开场白，抛出"被忽视的真相" |

**开场三连**：How are you doing today? → 寒暄完 *Guess what?* → 抛话题 *People don't really talk about…*

## 🗂 场景二：职场 / 干活 / 推动事情
| 表达 | 中文 | 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **recruit for a job** | 为某岗位招人 | We're recruiting for a new English teacher. |
| **dive into** | 一头扎进去、深入 | Let's dive into the details.（抽象用 into，❌ in） |
| **build out a toolbox** | 打造一整套本领 | toolbox = 可复用技能集合 |
| **want sb to do sth** | 想让某人做某事 | 不能省 to |
| **shit on (sb/sth)** | 贬低、臭骂 ⚠️粗俗 | 非正式带攻击性，职场慎用 |

## 🗂 场景三：表达立场 / 谈人生选择
| 表达 | 中文 | 关键用法 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **not for anything** | 无论如何也不、给啥都不换 | wouldn't … for anything，力度强于 not at all |
| **have gratitude for / be grateful for** | 对…心怀感恩 | 书面 gratitude，口语更常 be grateful for |
| **the + N + that comes with…** | 做某事随之而来的… | 后接动名词：the loneliness that comes with **choosing**… |
| **stay small / it serves them** | 保持不起眼 / 对他们有利 | serve sb = benefits them，点破别人动机 |
| **there's no way (that)…** | 绝不可能… | 语气最硬的否定之一 |

**立场陈述模板**：
> I wouldn't trade this for anything. I'm grateful for the toolbox that comes with it — but people don't really talk about the loneliness, and there's no way I'd go back.
