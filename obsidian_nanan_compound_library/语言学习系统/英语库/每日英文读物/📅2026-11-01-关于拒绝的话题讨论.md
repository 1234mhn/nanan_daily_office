## 一、原文全文（English Transcript）

As you were speaking, I was thinking about what's at the root of that for so many people. And one of the things that came to me was this fear of rejection, yes. I know so many people who will cancel on dates last minute because they're scared of, well, what if I go and it doesn't work out. Hmm, or rejection by the way is two types of rejection. One type of rejection is that person doesn't want me, and the other type of rejection is that person doesn't meet my expectations or that person I don't wanna be with them, yes. So I'm not just saying rejection in that we're scared of, well, what if that person doesn't like me, we're also scared of the "am I gonna reject another person" and like you said with the high expectations, what do we do about both of those types of our fear of rejection?

Because let's take the more obvious one to start with. We all want to be loved, we all want the next person to be the person, yeah, chances are that's not gonna happen, we know that, yeah. But still we struggle with the fact that I'm gonna meet someone and they're gonna say, "Well, you're not right for me," or you know, "I don't really wanna see you again." Like how do you get over that because we talk about it in entrepreneurship, we talk about it in life, but when you do it with love, it seems so personal. It's one thing to be rejected by the person you love, it's another thing to be rejected by a stranger who you don't know.

This is just human nature. We go on dates even if we're not into the person, we want them to be into us or right, yes. Or like we find them, let's say we find them attractive, and then we find out, wait, you're not into me, you don't find me attractive. Yeah, but life really, the quality of our lives is very much determined by how well we can confront rejection. And maybe we can just all acknowledge the awkwardness of it all. But you still have to, if you want love and you want a relationship, you have to go for it. You can't just be passive.

## 二、核心短语与句型

### 🔑 值得记的句型

1. **What's at the root of that for so many people.** — 对很多人来说，这背后的根源是什么。
   → 用于分析问题根源：*What's at the root of procrastination for so many students?*

2. **I'm not just saying A, we're also scared of B.** — 我不只是在说 A，我们同样害怕 B。
   → 补充常见误解/扩展话题：*I'm not just talking about money — I mean time too.*

3. **It's one thing to..., it's another thing to...** — ……是一回事，……又是另一回事。
   → 对比两件事的难度/性质：*It's one thing to know a word, it's another thing to use it.*

4. **Chances are (that)...** — 很有可能……（口语高频）
   → *Chances are he won't show up.*

5. **We all want the next person to be the person.** — 我们都希望下一个人就是对的人。
   → be **the** person = 命中注定的那个人。

6. **The quality of our lives is very much determined by how well we can...** — 我们生活的质量，很大程度上取决于我们有多能……
   → 万能议论句：*The quality of your life is determined by the quality of your questions.*

7. **I really believe this with all of my heart and soul.** — 我全身心都相信这一点。
   → 强调笃信（比 I really believe 更重）。

8. **They absolutely are not for you, I can guarantee that.** — 他绝对不适合你，我保证。
   → guarantee 用于给出强断言。

9. **I wish there was a magic pill I could give people to just get over it.** — 我希望有颗灵丹妙药能让人一步跨过去。
   → I wish + 过去式（虚拟语气），表“现实没有”。

10. **You have to be stronger than that.** — 你得比这更坚强。
    → 鼓励、鞭策他人。

11. **You can't just be passive.** — 你不能光被动等待。
    → 收尾式劝告。

## 三、场景说明

- **恋爱/约会场景**：be into sb、not feeling a spark、cancel on sb、it doesn't work out、not right for me
- **鼓励与自我调节**：put yourself out there、go for it、build resilience、get over it、be stronger than that
- **泛议论/观点表达**：It's one thing to..., it's another thing to...；Chances are...；The quality of our lives is determined by...
- **拒绝的两种类型（本文核心观点）**：
  1. 别人不要我（rejection by others）
  2. 我不要别人（rejecting others / 对方没达到我的期待）
